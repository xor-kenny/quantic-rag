# Equipment and Asset Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy covers company-owned equipment issued to employees of Northwind Systems, a fictional company used for coursework: what is provided, how it is requested, how it is maintained, and how it is returned. It applies to all employees and to contractors issued company equipment.

Equipment purchased with company funds remains company property throughout its life, regardless of who used it or for how long, and regardless of whether the purchase was reimbursed to the employee.

## Standard Issue

Every employee receives a company-managed laptop appropriate to their role. Engineering, data, and design roles receive the higher-specification model; other roles receive the standard model.

Employees may additionally request one external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, a headset, and a docking station. These are issued on request rather than automatically, because not every employee needs them.

Mobile phones are issued only to roles with an on-call obligation or a customer-facing requirement for a separate business number. Other employees who need occasional mobile access receive the published monthly service allowance instead; see the Business Expense and Equipment Policy.

| Item | Provided | Refresh cycle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laptop, standard | Automatically | 4 years |
| Laptop, high specification | Automatically for eligible roles | 3 years |
| External monitor | On request | 5 years |
| Keyboard, mouse, headset | On request | On failure |

## Requesting Equipment

Standard items are requested through the IT service portal and are normally fulfilled within five business days for employees at an office location, and within seven to ten business days where shipping to a home address is required.

Non-standard requests, meaning anything outside the table above, require a documented business need and manager approval. Requests above $500 additionally require Finance pre-approval before the order is placed.

Employees must not purchase computing equipment themselves and seek reimbursement afterwards. Personal laptops are not reimbursable without a written exception from IT and Finance, because a device purchased outside the managed procurement process cannot be brought into security compliance retroactively.

## Accessibility and Ergonomic Needs

Where an employee has an accessibility or ergonomic need, IT and Workplace Services source appropriate equipment directly rather than reimbursing a personal purchase. There is no cost to the employee and no charge against any team budget.

These requests are coordinated by People Operations and are handled confidentially. An employee is not required to disclose a medical diagnosis to their manager to obtain equipment; a statement of the functional need is sufficient.

An ergonomic assessment is available on request to any employee, whether office-based or remote.

## Care and Maintenance

Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of company equipment. Reasonable care means keeping devices in their possession while travelling, not leaving them unattended in a vehicle or public space, using the supplied protective case where one is issued, and not exposing equipment to obvious hazards.

Equipment must remain enrolled in device management. Employees may not disable endpoint monitoring, remove management software, alter disk encryption settings, or install an unapproved operating system.

Faults should be reported through the IT service portal. Employees must not attempt their own repairs or take a device to a third-party repair provider, because doing so can void warranty coverage and can compromise the device's security posture.

Ordinary wear and accidental damage are covered by the company and are not charged to the employee or their team. Damage arising from clear negligence, such as leaving a laptop on the roof of a car, may be reviewed with the manager and People Operations, though the company's normal practice is to replace the device and address the pattern rather than the incident.

## Loss and Theft

A lost or stolen device must be reported to Security immediately, and in any event within twenty-four hours of the employee becoming aware. Report through the security incident channel, not only to IT.

Security will remotely lock and, where warranted, wipe the device. Employees should not delay reporting in the hope that a device will reappear; a device recovered after a delayed report must still be treated as potentially compromised.

Where a theft has occurred, employees should file a police report and provide the reference number to Security, who will need it for the insurance claim.

A replacement device is issued as a priority once the incident is logged. Employees are not left without equipment while an incident is investigated.

## Software

Software is installed from the approved catalogue in the IT self-service portal. Software outside the catalogue requires an IT review covering licensing, security, and data handling before installation.

Employees may not install software from unverified sources, may not use a personal or trial licence for business work, and may not circumvent licence controls.

Browser extensions are software for the purposes of this policy. Extensions can read page content, including Confidential material, and are subject to the same review.

## Return on Separation or Transfer

All company equipment must be returned by the final day of employment. IT provides a prepaid shipping label to remote employees at least one week before the final day.

The return includes laptops, monitors, peripherals, docking stations, mobile phones, security keys, access badges, and any company-purchased home office furniture. Employees should back up nothing to personal storage before returning a device; personal files should be removed, not copied elsewhere.

Where equipment is not returned within fourteen days of the final day, People Operations follows up in writing. The company may pursue recovery of the equipment or its value where it remains unreturned, subject to the limits of applicable state law on payroll deduction.

An employee transferring between teams normally keeps their equipment. Where a transfer changes the role's equipment tier, IT arranges the exchange.

## Common Questions

**My laptop is four years old and slow. Can I get a new one?** Standard laptops refresh at four years. Raise a request through the IT service portal; if the device is at or past its refresh date, replacement is routine.

**Can I buy my own laptop and expense it?** No. Personal laptops are not reimbursable without a written exception from IT and Finance. Request a company-managed device instead.

**Can I keep my laptop when I leave?** No. All equipment is company property and must be returned by the final day, including any home office furniture purchased with company funds.

**I need a specific chair for a back condition.** Contact People Operations rather than buying it yourself. Accessibility and ergonomic equipment is sourced directly at no cost to you, and you do not need to disclose a diagnosis to your manager.

**My screen cracked when I dropped my bag.** Report it through the IT service portal. Accidental damage is covered; you will not be charged.

## Exceptions and Interpretation

IT owns this policy jointly with Workplace Services. Exceptions to the standard issue require manager and Finance approval. Exceptions to the security requirements require Security approval and are recorded in the exception register.
