# Onboarding and Access Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy describes what happens between an accepted offer and the end of an employee's first ninety days at Northwind Systems, a fictional company used for coursework. It covers pre-start preparation, first-week requirements, access provisioning, role training, and the checkpoints managers are expected to hold.

Onboarding is a shared responsibility. People Operations owns the process and the compliance requirements, the hiring manager owns role readiness, IT owns equipment and access, and the employee owns completing the tasks assigned to them within the stated deadlines.

## Before the Start Date

People Operations sends the pre-start packet once the background check has cleared. The packet contains the employment agreement, tax withholding forms, direct deposit setup, emergency contact collection, and the employee handbook acknowledgement.

IT ships a company-managed laptop to the address the employee provides, timed to arrive one to three business days before the start date. Employees who have not received equipment by the day before their start date should contact their recruiting coordinator rather than waiting.

The hiring manager prepares a thirty-sixty-ninety day plan and identifies an onboarding partner from the team. The onboarding partner is a peer, not a manager, and their role is to answer the ordinary questions that new employees are often reluctant to raise with a manager.

## First Week

New employees complete identity verification, security awareness training, benefits enrollment, and equipment acknowledgement in the first week. Managers schedule role training and introductions.

Identity verification must be completed within the first three business days as a legal requirement. Employees working remotely complete verification through the company's authorized remote verification partner.

Security awareness training must be completed before broad system access is granted. This is a hard gate: IT will not provision access to production systems, customer data, or the code repository until the training completion is recorded.

Benefits enrollment is time-limited. Newly eligible employees have thirty days from their eligibility notice to enroll; see the Benefits Eligibility Policy. Missing the window means waiting for the next open enrollment period or a qualifying life event.

## Access and Data Handling

Access is granted using least privilege. Passwords may not be shared. Suspected loss of a device or exposure of company information must be reported to Security immediately.

Least privilege means an employee receives the minimum access required to perform their role, and nothing further. Access requests beyond the role baseline require the manager's approval and, for systems holding customer data, the approval of the system owner.

Access is provisioned from a role template. Managers should review the template before the start date and flag anything missing, because retrofitting access after the fact takes longer than requesting it in advance.

Every employee receives a password manager licence during the first week and is expected to use it. Credentials may not be stored in a browser profile, a notes application, a spreadsheet, or a chat message. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts and may not be disabled.

Shared or team accounts are permitted only where a system genuinely cannot support individual accounts, and only with Security approval and a named owner responsible for rotating the credential.

## Role Training and Checkpoints

Managers schedule role-specific training during the first two weeks and hold a documented checkpoint at thirty days. The checkpoint covers what is going well, what is unclear, and whether the thirty-sixty-ninety plan needs adjusting.

A ninety-day review is held jointly by the manager and People Operations. The review is a two-way conversation and its outcome is recorded in the employee record. It is not a probationary decision point; employment at Northwind Systems is not conditioned on passing a probationary period.

New managers additionally complete the manager fundamentals programme within their first ninety days, covering performance conversations, the leave policies, and their obligations under the Workplace Conduct and Reporting Policy.

## Internal Transfers

An employee moving to a new team completes an abbreviated onboarding covering role training, access changes, and a thirty-day checkpoint with the new manager. Compliance training is not repeated unless the new role carries additional requirements.

Access from the previous role is revoked at the transfer date unless the employee has a documented need to retain it during a handover period, in which case retention is time-limited and approved by both managers.

## Common Questions

**My laptop has not arrived and I start Monday.** Contact your recruiting coordinator immediately. Do not use a personal device to begin work; personal devices may not be used to access company systems.

**Can I start with production access on day one?** No. Security awareness training gates access to production systems, customer data, and the code repository. Complete the training first.

**I missed the benefits enrollment window.** Contact the Benefits team. Outside a qualifying life event, the next opportunity is open enrollment.

**Do I need to complete training again after transferring teams?** Only if your new role carries requirements your previous role did not. Your manager and People Operations will confirm.

## Exceptions and Interpretation

People Operations owns this policy. Deadlines tied to legal requirements, in particular identity verification, cannot be extended. Other deadlines may be adjusted where an employee begins work immediately before a company holiday or a scheduled office closure.
