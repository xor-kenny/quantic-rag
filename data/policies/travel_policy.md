# Business Travel Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy governs business travel booked or reimbursed by Northwind Systems, a fictional company used for coursework. It applies to all employees and to contractors whose agreement provides for travel reimbursement.

This policy sits alongside the Business Expense and Equipment Policy, which governs documentation and submission, and the Remote Work and Location Policy, which governs where work may be performed. Travelling to a location and working from a location are different things: a business trip is not a temporary work location, and an extended personal stay attached to a business trip may require separate approval.

## Approval

All business travel requires manager pre-approval before booking, regardless of cost. International travel additionally requires approval from the employee's vice president.

Approval requests state the business purpose, the destination, the dates, and the estimated total cost. Approval covers the trip as described; a material change in destination, duration, or cost requires re-approval.

Travel to a country on the company's restricted destination list requires Security approval in addition to the approvals above. The list is maintained by Security and reflects both safety conditions and export-control considerations.

## Booking

Travel is booked through the company travel platform. Booking outside the platform requires a documented reason and is reimbursed at no more than the fare the platform would have offered for an equivalent itinerary.

The platform is not simply a cost control. It provides the traveller location data the company needs to reach employees during a disruption or emergency, which is the reason bookings made elsewhere must still be recorded in it.

Employees should book at least fourteen days in advance where the trip is foreseeable. Bookings inside seven days require the manager to confirm they accept the higher fare.

## Air Travel

Economy class is the standard for all flights. Premium economy is permitted for flights with a scheduled duration over six hours. Business class is permitted only for flights over ten hours, and only with vice president approval obtained before booking.

Employees may choose a fare up to $150 above the lowest logical fare where doing so meaningfully improves the itinerary, such as avoiding an overnight layover or a second connection. Larger differences require the manager's agreement.

Seat selection fees, one checked bag, and standard boarding are reimbursable. Priority boarding, lounge access, and seat upgrades are personal upgrades and are not reimbursable.

Employees retain personal frequent-flyer benefits earned on business travel. Employees may not select a more expensive itinerary to earn status, and loyalty programme membership fees are not reimbursable.

## Lodging

Employees should book accommodation within the platform's displayed limit for the destination city. Where no suitable room is available within the limit, the platform records the exception automatically.

Room service and in-room entertainment are not reimbursable. Hotel internet is reimbursable. Laundry is reimbursable on trips of five nights or more.

Employees attending a conference should book the conference rate where it is available, even where it exceeds the city limit.

Staying with family or friends instead of booking a hotel is permitted. A modest gift or meal for the host, up to $100 per trip, is reimbursable in place of the room cost.

## Ground Transport

Standard-class rail is the expected mode where it is competitive with flying on total journey time. Rail bookings follow the same advance-booking guidance as air travel.

Rideshare, taxi, and public transport are reimbursable for business journeys. Rental cars require manager approval and are limited to standard-class vehicles unless the number of travellers or the equipment being carried requires otherwise.

Employees driving a personal vehicle for business are reimbursed at the published mileage rate, which covers fuel and running costs. Parking and tolls are reimbursable separately. Ordinary commuting between home and the assigned office is never reimbursable, including on a day involving business travel later.

## Meals

Meals during business travel are reimbursable at actual cost with an itemised receipt for any expense of $25 or more. Northwind Systems does not use a fixed daily allowance.

Alcohol is not reimbursable in any category, including during a client meal or a conference dinner.

Where a meal is provided as part of a conference registration or by a host, a separate meal at company expense is not reimbursable for that sitting.

## Personal Travel Combined with Business

Employees may extend a business trip for personal reasons at their own cost. The company pays no more than the cost of the business-only itinerary; the employee should obtain a comparison quote at the time of booking and attach it to the expense claim.

Days spent on personal travel are recorded as paid time off. Approval for a business trip is not approval for the personal days attached to it, which follow the normal PTO request process.

Working from the destination after the business purpose has ended converts the stay into a temporary work location and is governed by the Remote Work and Location Policy. A personal extension of more than ten business days in another state, or any personal extension abroad during which the employee works, requires the approvals set out in that policy.

## Safety and Duty of Care

The company maintains a travel assistance programme covering medical referral, emergency evacuation, and security support. Details and the twenty-four-hour contact number are in the travel platform.

Employees must keep their itinerary current in the platform so that the company can locate them during a disruption. This is the single most important operational requirement in this policy.

Employees who feel unsafe at a destination should contact their manager and Security and should not continue the trip pending guidance. No employee is expected to complete a trip they reasonably believe to be unsafe.

Travel insurance is provided through the company programme. Individually purchased travel insurance is not reimbursable.

## Common Questions

**Can I fly business class on a nine-hour flight?** No. Business class requires a flight over ten hours and vice president approval before booking. Premium economy is available for flights over six hours.

**I want to stay a week extra for a holiday.** Book the extension at your own cost, attach a comparison quote showing the business-only fare, and request PTO for the personal days. If you plan to work during the extension, the Remote Work and Location Policy applies.

**Can I expense the wine at the client dinner?** No. Subtract the alcohol from the claimed amount and note the adjustment.

**I booked directly with the airline because it was cheaper.** Attach the comparison and the reason. You will be reimbursed up to the platform fare, and you must still record the itinerary in the platform for duty-of-care purposes.

**Is my drive to the airport reimbursable?** Yes, at the published mileage rate, along with airport parking. Your normal commute to the office is not.

## Exceptions and Interpretation

Finance owns this policy jointly with Workplace Services. Exceptions require the approval of the traveller's manager and Finance, obtained before booking. Safety-related exceptions may be approved by Security at any time.
